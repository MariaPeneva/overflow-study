Overflow toolbar - Study
Research Study

Help us improve the Overflow toolbar

We're exploring a menu-based overflow behavior for the Overflow toolbar. This is a change to the way the overflow works, not only a cosmetic update. Before we commit to a direction, we want to understand how your Line of Business (LoB) uses it today and what this change would mean for your product.
